DOMPurify 3.4.0 is used under its **Apache-2.0 alternative** for this distribution. Its original complete Apache-2.0 / MPL-2.0 dual-license notice remains in `assets/vendor/licenses/mermaid-bundled/dompurify-3.4.0-LICENSE`.

Third-party components retain their own licenses. MIT, BSD, ISC, Apache-2.0 and OFL copyright, permission and disclaimer texts are retained in `assets/vendor/licenses/` and `assets/fonts/LICENSE-OFL.txt`; the component/file inventory is `assets/resource-licenses.json`. Fonts are not relicensed under the JavaScript libraries' licenses.

The original GPLv2 text remains in `LICENSE`; the GPLv3 text selected for this distribution is in `LICENSE-GPL-3.0.txt`. Applicable corresponding-source, modification-notice and attribution obligations must still be satisfied. This route selection does not turn an unresolved release check into a pass.

This attribution does not relicense the package or assert a separately obtained grant from that page. The distribution relies on the upstream MIT grant; the owner has confirmed this treatment for 0.10.12.
